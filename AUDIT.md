# Recipe audit, September 2026

Every recipe was checked for ratios, times, yields, pan sizes, food safety and shopping totals. Sources for adapted recipes were found and verified by fetching the page. Changes are also shown on each recipe card in the app under "Checked in the audit".

## Fixed: would have failed or misled

| Recipe | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Savoury scones | 2 Tbsp baking powder for 6 cups flour is half the Edmonds ratio. Flat scones. | 4 Tbsp |
| Date scones | Same: 1 Tbsp for 3 cups. | 2 Tbsp |
| Mini mince pies | One box of Pillsbury crust (two 9 in rounds) cuts 8 bases of 11 cm, not 12. 11 cm also leaves no lip to seal in a muffin cup. | 2 boxes; cut 12 cm bases, re-rolling scraps |
| Pasta bake | 500 g rigatoni + two 28 oz cans + veg + 24 meatballs is about 5 L. Two 8×8 pans hold 4 L. The sauce pot also put five pans on four burners on Day 3. | 350 g rigatoni, sauce is 1 L of Day 2 bolognese, no separate tomato sauce |
| Bolognese | Yield said 3 L; the ingredients make about 3.8 L, so the portioning line was wrong. A 5.5 qt pot cannot take 5.2 L raw. | Yield 3.8 L split 2.2 L lasagne / 1 L pasta bake / rest bagged; pot 7 qt+; ice bath before the fridge |
| Sausage rolls, Day 1 | Puff pastry bought at 08:30 and rolled at 11:20 has 80 min to thaw. It needs about 4 h. | Pastry into the fridge Friday night (new first step on Day 1) |
| Sausage rolls | 35–40 min from frozen is marginal for a 3 cm meat core. | 40–45 min, 71 °C / 160 °F centre |
| Breakfast burritos | Filling was about 10 cups for 12 tortillas that hold 8. | 8 eggs instead of 12 |
| Shopping list | Tortillas were missing entirely (24 needed). | Added a 30-pack |
| Shopping list | 8×8 foil pans: 4 listed, 5 needed (enchiladas 2, pasta bake 2, shepherd's pie 1). | 6 |
| Shopping list | Tomato paste: 10 Tbsp needed, one 4.5 oz tube holds 8. | 2 tubes or a 6 oz can |
| Shopping list | Black beans: 4 cans needed (salad uses 2), 3 listed. | 4 |

## Fixed: quality and clarity

| Recipe | Change |
|---|---|
| Dairy-free lasagne | White sauce raised from 5 to 6 cups oat milk (100 g flour). 5 cups was about a quarter short for 8 pans with the layers as written. |
| Pork and fennel meatballs | Salt 2 tsp → 1 Tbsp fine salt (about 1% of the mix). "Heaped Tbsp" → "golf-ball size" for a 30 g ball. |
| Anzac biscuits | Flour "1 cup (150 g)" → 125 g. Every other card uses 125 g per cup; 150 g would make a dry biscuit. |
| PB oat bites | Honey ⅓ → ½ cup so the balls bind without extra milk. |
| Savoury mince, Day 2 | Salt stated (1½ tsp). Finishes about 10:45, not 10:25. Pie portion chills in the freezer for 20 min so it is cold for the 11:15 pies. |
| Day 2 cooling | Meatballs and roast vegetables go into the fridge at noon instead of sitting out until 13:00. Bolognese gets a 20 min ice bath and shallow tubs. |
| Day 2 roasting | Three trays across two racks instead of one overloaded tray, which would steam rather than roast. |
| Day 3 stove | Enchilada sauce before the filling; rigatoni last; pasta bake bolognese warmed in the microwave. |
| Sous vide | Bags in a single layer, no thicker than 3 cm, so the centre reaches temperature in the stated time. Breast time 90 min → 2 h. |
| Enchiladas | Wrap pairs in foil so two can be pulled out at a time. Note that rolled 8 in tortillas ride up the sides of an 8×8 pan. |
| Butter chicken | Sear in 3 batches, not 2. |
| Reheat guide | Microwave times raised for dense frozen blocks: pasta bake and shepherd's pie 6–8 min, butter chicken 5–6, soup 3 + 3, bolognese bag +2–3, mince pies in the air fryer 15–18 min. Added "stir, and check it is steaming hot in the middle". |
| Bean salads | Fridge life 4–5 days → 3–4 days (USDA, cooked chicken). |
| Shopping list | Added muffin liners, cocoa, parmesan, freezer tubs, quart bags 30+, green bell pepper, vinegar/sugar/white pepper. Chicken stock 3 cartons (was exactly 64 oz for 64 oz). Oat milk 4 cartons. Lemons 6. Frozen corn 2 lb. Dates 150 g (PB bites have none). Rotisserie chicken bought Saturday 20, not Sunday. Egg bites marked as containing cheese. Worcestershire noted as containing anchovy. |
| Wording | Facts card said "9 dishes, 2 marked if room"; there are 10 and only the pasta bake is optional. Glossary notes beef shepherd's pie is strictly cottage pie. "Every recipe works without dairy" now says "every home-made recipe" because the Costco egg bites contain cheese. |

## Checked and left alone

Banana oat muffins, corn muffins, breakfast sandwiches (egg sheet, toast-first, paper towel), stroganoff (braise time, flour to stock), shepherd's pie proportions, butter chicken quantities and salt, pumpkin soup yield, freezer rice ratio, sous vide temperatures, oven temperature sequences on all three days, freezer-life claims (USDA windows), and the cross-day dependencies (everything used on Day 3 is made on Day 1 or 2).

Well-known versions (RecipeTin Eats, Cookie and Kate, Downshiftology) were checked and differ enough that crediting them would be wrong.
