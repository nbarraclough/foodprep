The October Freezer

Freezer plan for the baby due 4 October. Three cook days, about 48 dinners and 230 snacks. Everything reheats in the microwave or air fryer. Nothing is spicy, there is no fish, and every recipe works without dairy. Cheese is optional and marked.

Bean salads, one a week

Not for the freezer. Make one on Sunday night; it keeps 4–5 days in the fridge and tastes better on day 2. No lettuce, so it does not go soggy. Eat from the tub or in a warm tortilla. Method is Violet Witchel's.

Formula: 2 cans beans (two kinds, rinsed) + 1 protein + 3 crunchy vegetables cut bean-sized + 1 pickled or briny thing + a whole bunch of herbs + dressing (3 parts oil to 1 part acid, plus mustard, garlic, salt). Mix, taste, fridge.
Chicken for salads: 800 g thighs or breasts, salt, garlic powder, lemon zest, sealed. Sous vide 63 °C / 145 °F for 90 min (breast) or 74 °C / 165 °F for 2 h (thigh). Chill in the bag, dice cold. Or use a Costco rotisserie chicken.

Cook days

Ordered so the oven changes temperature as few times as possible. Times are for two people; add an hour for one. Ticks save on this device.

Shopping list

Every recipe added up, with a small buffer. One Costco run before Day 1, a grocery top-up before Day 2. Chicago store names are a guide; swap for whatever is close.
